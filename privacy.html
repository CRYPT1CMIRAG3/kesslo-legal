NutriScan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

NutriScan ("the app", "we") gives you a personal score for packaged food from a photo of its label. The app is made by Nikhil Anand, Dubai, United Arab Emirates, who is responsible for your data ("data controller"). Contact: contact.nutri.scan.fit@gmail.com.

The short version:

  • By default, everything stays on your phone. The AI that reads labels runs on your phone. Your profile and scans are stored encrypted on your phone. You don't need an account.
  • Photos are never stored. Not on your phone, not on our server.
  • Some things you can switch on send data off your phone: an account (to use NutriScan on several phones), Cloud AI (for phones that are too slow for on-device AI), crash reports, and purchases. Free users also see a banner ad. Each is explained below.
  • We don't sell your data, we don't show personalised ads, and we never give your health details to advertisers.

1. What the app keeps on your phone

Data Why
Your profile: age, sex, height, weight, unit system, activity level, goals, allergies, diets, health conditions, pregnancy or breastfeeding To work out your daily targets and score food for you
Your scans: the text the AI read from the label, product name, nutrients, score, flags and reasons So you can see your history
Settings (theme, chosen AI model, crash report choice, Cloud AI consent) To remember your choices
An app log (event names and timings, no label text or health details) So you can copy it and send it to us by hand if something goes wrong

This data is stored in an encrypted database (AES-256) whose key is kept in your phone's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). It is excluded from phone backups. We can't see it unless you choose one of the options below. You can export it (Settings › Export my data) or delete it (Settings › Delete all data) at any time. Uninstalling the app also deletes it.

Label photos you take or pick are kept in memory only while the scan runs. The temporary file from the camera or photo picker is deleted as soon as it has been read. Photos are never saved to your gallery, app storage or any server.

2. Data that leaves your phone, and who receives it

2.1 AI model download (Hugging Face)

The first time you scan with an on-device model, the app downloads the model file from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). This is an ordinary file download: Hugging Face sees your IP address and that the file was downloaded, but no profile data, photos or identifiers are sent. If you use your phone's built-in AI (Gemini Nano on Android, Apple Intelligence on iPhone) nothing is downloaded and everything runs on the phone.

2.2 Account and sync (Supabase) — optional

If you create an account, the app stores in your account:

  • your email address and an encrypted (hashed) password, or, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the account id, email address and name that Google or Apple share with us;
  • your user id (a random id created for your account);
  • a copy of your profile, your scans (text only, never photos) and your theme setting, so they appear on your other phones.

Before you sign in, the app tells you that your health profile and scan history will be stored in your account. By signing in you give your explicit consent to this.

The account is run on Supabase (Supabase Inc.), our hosting provider, on servers in Mumbai, India (AWS ap-south-1). Supabase encrypts the data in transit and at rest and processes it only on our instructions. Your data can only be read through your own signed-in session; Supabase's and our administrators can technically access the database, and we only do so to keep the service running or when the law requires it. The data is not end-to-end encrypted. Supabase keeps short-lived security logs of sign-ins (including IP address).

When you create an account, sign in or reset your password, the app runs an invisible Cloudflare Turnstile check (Cloudflare, Inc.) to stop bots. Cloudflare sees technical details about your device and connection for this check only. Cloudflare's handling of this data is described in the Turnstile Privacy Addendum: https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/

2.3 Cloud AI (Google Gemini) — optional

If your phone is too slow for on-device AI, you can pick Cloud AI. It needs an account, and the app asks for your agreement once on each phone before it is used. With Cloud AI, each scan or re-scan sends:

  • the label photos (or, for a re-scan, the label text saved earlier), and
  • the parts of your profile used for scoring: age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goals, allergies, diets, health conditions and pregnancy or breastfeeding,

over an encrypted connection to NutriScan's server (a Supabase Edge Function), which passes them to Google Gemini (Google LLC) to score the label and returns the result to your phone. Your name and email are not sent to Google. We use Google's paid Gemini API, under which Google does not use the content to train or improve its models. Google may keep requests for a limited time to detect abuse, as its terms allow.

Our server does not store or log the photos, the profile details or the result. It only records, per account, how many Cloud AI scans you used each day, and logs the time taken and size of each request.

Free Cloud AI scans. Without a Cloud AI subscription you get one free Cloud AI scan a week per phone. To count it, the app sends an id for your phone (on Android the system's app-specific Android ID; on iPhone a random id the app stores in your Keychain). The server stores only a one-way, salted hash of that id together with the week and the number of free scans used. It is not linked to your account.

2.4 Purchases (Google Play, App Store and RevenueCat)

You can buy Remove ads (one time) or subscribe to Cloud AI. Payments are handled by Google Play or Apple's App Store; we never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA) to check what you have bought. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and receipts from the store, a random app user id and, if you are signed in, your NutriScan user id (so our server can check your Cloud AI subscription), plus basic device information such as the app version, OS version, country and IP address. We do not send RevenueCat your email, profile or scans.

2.5 Ads (Google AdMob) — free users only

If you haven't bought Remove ads or Cloud AI, the app shows a banner ad at the bottom of the Home and History screens only. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). We ask only for non-personalised ads, which are chosen from the app and general context, not from a profile of you. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK collects data such as your IP address (used for approximate location), the advertising id of your phone, device and app information, and how you interact with the ads. We never give AdMob your health profile, scans, label text or photos.

  • On iPhone the app does not ask to track you, and NutriScan does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • In the EU, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before any ad loads; you can change your choice in Settings › Remove ads & Cloud AI › Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising id in your phone's settings.
  • Paying users never load the ad SDK.

See how Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

2.6 Crash reports (Sentry) — optional, off by default

If you switch on Send anonymous crash reports (during setup or in Settings › Privacy), the app sends a report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., USA) when it crashes. A report holds only the error type, the stack trace, the app version, OS version and phone model. Error messages, screenshots, breadcrumbs, user and request data are removed before sending, and no user id is attached. Reports never include your health details, label text or scores.

2.7 What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not use your data for personalised advertising.
  • We do not share your health details with anyone except, when you choose Cloud AI, Google Gemini as described above.

5. Your choices and rights

In the app you can, at any time:

  • See and export your data: Settings › Export my data (JSON file).
  • Correct it: edit your profile in Settings.
  • Delete one scan, or everything: Settings › Delete all data (this also empties your account if you are signed in).
  • Delete your account: Settings › Account › Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything in it, and the data on that phone.

Deleting your account without the app. If you no longer have the app, email contact.nutri.scan.fit@gmail.com from the address of your account with the subject "Delete my NutriScan account". We will delete the account and all its data within 30 days and confirm by email. Purchases are kept by Google or Apple as their own records; cancel a subscription in your Play or App Store account (deleting the NutriScan account does not cancel it).

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, India, California and other US states), you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, and to nominate someone to act for you. To use any of these rights, email contact.nutri.scan.fit@gmail.com. We will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in India, the Data Protection Board of India; in the UK, the ICO; in the EU, your local supervisory authority).

6. Security

Data on your phone is encrypted and can be protected with an app lock (fingerprint or face). All connections use HTTPS. Account data can only be read with your own signed-in session (row-level security), passwords are stored only as hashes by Supabase, and our server's secret keys are never in the app. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

7. International transfers

Account data is stored in India. Google (Gemini, AdMob), RevenueCat, Sentry and Cloudflare may process data in the United States and other countries. Where EU/UK law applies, these transfers are covered by the providers' standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

8. Children

NutriScan is for people aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has created an account, email contact.nutri.scan.fit@gmail.com and we will delete it. Users under 18 should use the app with a parent's or guardian's agreement where local law requires it.

9. Health information disclaimer

NutriScan's scores are AI estimates to help you compare food. They are not medical advice. Always check the label yourself, especially for allergens.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is significant (for example a new kind of data or a new provider).
